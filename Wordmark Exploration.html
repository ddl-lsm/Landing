Reading — recommendation

What I'd ship and why. The rest stay as named alternates we can swap into context.

Lead pair

Wordmark 01 Manrope ExtraBold lowercase

Monogram B Filled amber tile

Geometric, modern, confident — sharp like keen, calm like dwell. Sits cleanly next to the existing amber symbol without competing. Reads at 14px in the nav and at 24px on a business card. The monogram works as favicon, app icon, social avatar without modification.

Named alternates

  • 02Instrument Serif Italic — for case-study covers and long-form editorial. Plays the Dwell-magazine adjacency.
  • 04IBM Plex Mono — reserved for code, specs, BOM tables and API surfaces only. Never the primary wordmark.
  • 05Stacked uppercase — print covers, posters, foil-stamped folios. One-off applications.
  • 06Fraunces hybrid — premium marketing collateral when we need warmth without breaking the system.
  • 03Mid-dot Space Grotesk — secondary contexts, footer-scale typography.
