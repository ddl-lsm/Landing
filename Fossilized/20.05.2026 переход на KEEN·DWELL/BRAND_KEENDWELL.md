## 1. What the name actually means

KeenDwell is a compound. Both halves earn their place. The brand works on multiple semantic layers simultaneously — surface readers get one meaning, engineers get another, architects get a third.

### 1.1 "Keen" — full spectrum

| Register | Meaning | Why it matters to us |
|---|---|---|
| **Etymological** (Old English *cēne*) | Brave, bold, daring — warrior vocabulary in OE epic poetry | Hidden depth. Not SaaS-cliché, not jargon. Gives the brand quiet bravery. |
| **Sharp (physical)** | Having an acute edge — "a keen blade", "keen edge" | Precision, engineering edge |
| **Sharp (mental)** | Perceptive, acute — "keen intellect", "keen eye for detail" | Direct fit — engineering practice attentive to detail |
| **Sharp (sensory)** | Acute senses — "keen sense of smell", "keen hearing" | Sensors, perception — literally what a smart home is |
| **Eager** | Strongly interested (British English) — "keen on", "keen to help" | Warm human layer. Resonates with UK/EU audience especially. |
| **Intense** | Strong, piercing — "keen disappointment", "keen wind" | Literary register, adds weight |
| **Commercial** (Br. Eng.) | Sharply competitive — "keen prices" | Incidental but not harmful |
| **Verb *to keen*** (Irish/Scottish, from Gaelic *caoine*) | To wail in mourning | ⚠️ Dark note. Very narrow audience picks it up. **Avoid "we keen for…" phrasing in copy.** |

### 1.2 "Dwell" — full spectrum

| Register | Meaning | Why it matters to us |
|---|---|---|
| **Live, reside** | "dwell in a house" | Direct hit for smart-home. Warm word, not "property" or "real estate". |
| **Etymology** (OE *dwellan*) | To linger, remain in place | Rootedness, staying — opposite of disposable tech |
| **Focus deeply** | "dwell on details" | Sustained attention — engineering vocabulary |
| **Noun: dwelling** | Residence (formal / architectural) | Register of architects and developers — our B2B audience |
| **🔥 Dwell time** (engineering term) | Active in 5 engineering disciplines: | **Goldmine.** Hidden double-engineering meaning. |
| | Cam mechanism: follower's rest period | Mechanical engineering |
| | Ignition (dwell angle) | Automotive engineering |
| | Manufacturing: pause between operations | Production engineering |
| | UX analytics: time on page | Digital product engineering |
| | Cybersecurity: undetected attacker presence time | Software / security engineering |
| **Dwell (magazine)** | Premium US architecture publication since 2000 — bible of modern residential design | **Most important cultural adjacency.** Our ICP reads Dwell. KeenDwell lives in its orbit at no cost. |

### 1.3 The composite — what each audience hears

Layer by layer:

1. **Surface (0.3 sec):** "sharp + dwelling" → smart, perceptive home
2. **Educated reader (1–2 sec):** "keen eye + dwelling" → attentive residential design
3. **Engineer:** "keen + dwell time" → precision + system timing characteristics. Double-engineering insider read.
4. **Architect / designer:** Adjacency to Dwell magazine → premium residential architecture
5. **Literary reader:** OE bravery + acuity → a home that is bold and attentive

This **multi-layered legibility** is what single-word candidates (outpost, bastion) couldn't deliver.

## 2. Phonetics

- **/kiːn dwɛl/** — two stressed monosyllables. Spondee (DA-DAH). Strong rhythm.
- **/k/ + /d/** — voiceless + voiced plosives. Crisp attack on each syllable.
- **/iː/** in *Keen* — brightest, highest, frontmost English vowel. Energising.
- **/dw/** cluster — soft, rounded /w/ tempers the attack. Built-in warmth.
- **/ɛl/** ending — open, warm. Not clipped.
- Arc: **sharp opening → warm closing.** Engineering + home in the sound itself.
- No gum-brand collisions (Orbit / Span / Wrigley). No mushy syllables (intelli-).

## 3. Visual & typographic facts

- **9 letters, 4+5** — compact, scannable
- **KD monogram** — two angular caps, excellent favicon / app icon / drafting stamp
- **No descenders, one ascender (d)** — flat baseline, any typeface sits cleanly
- **Lockup considerations:**
  - `keendwell.com` — domain only
  - Wordmark recommended forms: `keendwell` (all lower) or `Keen·Dwell` (mid-dot divider) or `Keen Dwell` (split)
  - **CamelCase `KeenDwell` reads as 2014-era startup** — use only when single-word lockup is required (URLs, code, social handles)
- **Color:** Wordmark in amber `#EEB825` (existing brand) on dark `#0c0c0e`, or in `#F5F2EC` (warm off-white) on the amber when inverted
- **Logo mark** (existing amber symbol — `LOGO/logo-dark.png`) **NEVER recreated in SVG.** Always referenced as PNG. See `memory/feedback_use_existing_assets.md`.

## 4. Positioning — one sentence

> **Engineering precision applied to the place you live — remotely, attentively, with no heavy hardware in your way.**

- **"Keen"** = sharp engineering competence + attentive client service
- **"Dwell"** = dwelling (not property/system/object) + dwell-time (engineering precision in temporal parameters)

**Inverted summary:** We make a home smart while keeping it a home. Sharp mind — warm heart.

## 5. Voice & tone

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Concrete outcomes ("KNX project, 280 m², Berlin, $4,200, 3 weeks") | Vague claims ("the future of living") |
| British-leaning crispness ("a keener kind of dwelling") | American hyperbole ("revolutionary smart-home platform") |
| Engineering register where it earns trust (BOM, commissioning, VPN) | Engineer-jargon as decoration |
| Premium understatement | Decorative emoji, exclamation marks |
| "Remote", "remotely", "without site visit" — own the differentiator | Generic "digital transformation" SaaS-speak |
| Architecture vocabulary (dwelling, residence, building) where appropriate | "Property", "real estate", "solutions" |

## 7. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| "Dwell on" idiom can mean unproductive obsession ("don't dwell on it") | Low | Don't use "don't dwell" or negative-frame *dwell on* in copy |
| Irish *keening* = mourning wail | Very low (micro-audience) | Don't use verb "to keen" in copy |
| Adjacency to Dwell magazine (could read as imitation) | Low — more halo than harm | Never imitate Dwell's typography or layout. Own typeface, own rhythm. |
| CamelCase dated | Cosmetic | Wordmark uses lowercase or split form. CamelCase only for code/handles. |
| "Keen" reads British in US | Micro | Optionally use "sharp" alongside in US-targeted copy |
